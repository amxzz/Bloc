================================================================================
BLOC. GELATO — 2/12: MILK SERIES FORMULATION RECIPES
Standard Production Batch: 3.000 g
Base Archetype: Fior di Latte / White Milk Base
================================================================================

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
SOP PARAMETER PRODUKSI (STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. BALANCING     : Penimbangan presisi bahan kering & cair.
2. PASTEURIZING  : Pemanasan & pasteurisasi mix gelato.
3. RAPID COOLING : Penurunan suhu cepat ke 4°C dalam 10–15 menit.
4. AGING         : 4°C selama 4–12 jam.
5. CHURNING      : Suhu outlet -5°C s/d -9°C (durasi ±15–20 menit).
6. BLAST FREEZER : Suhu -30°C s/d -40°C (durasi ±30–45 menit).
7. TEMPERING     : Suhu inti -11°C s/d -14°C (durasi ±15–30 menit).
8. SHOWCASE      : Suhu display -14°C s/d -16°C (monitoring real-time).
